= Proof Status — SR-71 BlackGlider

== Summary

Two ledgers are tracked here and must not be conflated:

. *Formal (Idris2)* — the machine-checked constitutional companion, gated by
  `verification/proofs/idris2/MANIFEST` and verified by
  `bash scripts/check-proofs.sh idris2`.
. *Executable exhaustion (Rust)* — finite-domain proofs by exhaustive check
  (`tests/foundation.rs` C1–C7, `tests/constitutional.rs` C8–C13), verified by
  `cargo test`. These are complete over their stated finite domains (all 512
  local rule configurations; all 65,536 4×4 worlds against an independent
  oracle) but are not formal proofs over unbounded domains. See
  `PROOF-ROADMAP.md` for exactly what each one establishes.
