== AI implementation protocol

An AI assistant must read, in order:

. `0-AI-MANIFEST.a2ml`
. `.machine_readable/descriptiles/CLADE.a2ml`
. `.machine_readable/descriptiles/anchors/ANCHOR.a2ml`
. `.machine_readable/descriptiles/AGENTIC.a2ml`
. `.machine_readable/descriptiles/ECOSYSTEM.a2ml`
. `.machine_readable/descriptiles/STATE.a2ml`
. `MATHEMATICAL-CONTRACT.md`
. `VERIFICATION.md`

It must preserve the one-way authority boundary: presentation may observe the
canonical world but cannot change it. It must not add a heading assignment,
player-cell mutation, post-launch stamping, splicing, variable neighbourhood or
physics-bearing visual metric.

Any new physical claim requires executable evidence in the verification ledger.
A red claim remains red; gates must not be weakened to accept an implementation.

== Privacy

The project requires no personal data, telemetry, cloud account or uploaded
prompt history for local development. Do not send source, local paths, mission
designs or test results to an external AI service unless the maintainer has
knowingly chosen that service and its privacy terms. Never place credentials,
tokens, private conversation logs or model session state in the repository.

== Verification

Successful setup means `just test` and `just quality` both exit zero. It does not
mean the wider project is playable or release-ready; consult `VERIFICATION.md`
for the exact claims currently earned.
