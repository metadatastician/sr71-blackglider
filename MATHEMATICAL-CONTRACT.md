# Mathematical contract

## Constitutional claim

For one fixed initial finite pattern \(G_0\), boundary condition \(B\), and
canonical generation number \(t\), SR-71 BlackGlider's complete physical state
is

\[
G_t=\operatorname{Life}_{B}^{,t}(G_0).
\]

Rendering, textures, camera state, local spacetime embedding, analytical
detectors and post-launch input are not parameters of this equation.

## The rule

For each canonical cell with previous state \(s\in\{0,1\}\) and live-neighbour
count \(n\in\{0,\ldots,8\}\),

\[
s'=1\iff n=3\lor(s=1\land n=2).
\]

The neighbourhood is the fixed eight-cell Moore neighbourhood. All updates are
synchronous. The current reference implementation uses a finite rectangular
world with permanently dead exterior cells.

## Permitted operations

Before generation zero:

- select and construct a documented complete initial pattern;
- include gliders, reflectors, oscillators and control circuitry;
- choose a launch lane, phase or one of several complete initial conditions.

After generation zero:

- apply the B3/S23 transition;
- observe and classify patterns;
- evolve an independent no-launch counterfactual by the same rule;
- pause or change wall-clock display rate;
- change camera, projection, surface, texture, lighting and sound;
- annotate or predict without writing back to canonical cells.

## Forbidden operations

- player-specific movement, survival or update rules;
- imposed anchors, headings or phases;
- stamping, deleting, rotating or splicing cells after generation zero;
- choosing a different evolution table in response to live input;
- changing neighbourhoods because rendered surfaces become close or distant;
- regional update rates or physical time dilation in the canonical automaton;
- letting altitude, texture, slope, camera or curvature affect births or deaths;
- describing a coordinate warp as the cause of an objective turn.

## Geometry

The renderer may choose a time-dependent embedding

\[
\Phi_t:\mathbb Z^2\rightarrow\mathbb R^3.
\]

It may change lengths, angles, height and extrinsic curvature. It does not
change the canonical causal graph. Every rendered live surface corresponds to a
canonical live-cell event \((x,y,t)\).

The initial flight chart is

\[
X=(x+y)/2,\qquad Y=(x-y)/2,
\]

with inverse \(x=X+Y\), \(y=X-Y\) on the transformed lattice. Its parity
structure is retained; it is not silently resampled into a different automaton.

## Turns

A **frame manoeuvre** changes \(\Phi_t\) or the camera while leaving \(G_t\)
unchanged. It may feel like banking or pitching but cannot change relative
encounters in the canonical world.

A **Conway turn** is an objective change in a causal glider's lattice heading.
It is accepted only when the complete intermediate reaction appears in
successive raw states generated by B3/S23. Reflectors and collisions qualify;
heading assignment does not.

## Identity

Individual live cells are not persistent material particles. BlackGlider
identity is causal. Let \(S_0\) be the same initial condition without the launch
signal and evolve

\[
S_t=\operatorname{Life}_{B}^{,t}(S_0).
\]

The comparison \(D_t=G_t\oplus S_t\) attributes effects of the launch. It is an
analysis of two valid histories, not a third cellular automaton. An outgoing
glider may be called the BlackGlider when it is a localized causal descendant
absent from the no-launch history.

## Proof scope

The contract distinguishes proof from measurement:

- the 512-case local transition check is exhaustive for the implemented local
  rule;
- an independent bitmask implementation agrees with the kernel for every one of
  the 65,536 finite 4-by-4 worlds for one generation;
- all 16 glider heading/phase representatives are checked for detection and
  their expected four-generation translation;
- exact canonical serialization and a committed reaction certificate make the
  demonstrated finite history reproducible;
- Rust encapsulation and immutable borrowing establish capability boundaries;
- `verification/proofs/idris2/BlackGlider/Constitution.idr` constructively
  proves the constitutional equations it defines: all 18 legal centre-state
  and Moore-neighbour-count cases refine a separately stated bounded B3/S23
  specification, along with the four chart-axis mappings, deterministic
  iteration, observer exclusion from the evolution signature, and the declared
  causal-radius function;
- that Idris2 companion is not yet a refinement proof of `src/life.rs`; the
  agreement between model and implementation is currently supported by review
  plus executable tests, not by extraction or a verified foreign-function
  boundary;
- finite reaction and mission claims are exhaustive only over their declared
  lanes, phases, generations and boundaries;
- no finite test is described as an eternal theorem about the infinite plane;
- any universal claim not established remains explicitly unclaimed.
